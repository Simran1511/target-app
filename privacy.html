TARGET privacy policy

TARGET is a personal Windows app for logging daily progress on a study roadmap. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. Nothing you enter is ever sent to the app's maker or to anyone else.

What stays on your computer

Everything you enter is saved in a file in your Windows user folder. Dated backups are kept next to it. If you do not turn on sync, your entries never leave your computer. The only other internet use is opening links in your browser and checking whether links still work when you ask.

What sync does, if you turn it on

You sign in on Google's own page and choose the account yourself. TARGET never sees your password. It then saves a copy of your entries in a folder named "TARGET sync" in that account's Google Drive, so that your other computers can read it.

What TARGET can access in your Google account

TARGET asks for one permission, the drive.file scope. It lets TARGET see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive. It reads your email address only to show you which account is connected.

The sign-in is kept on your computer, encrypted so that only your Windows user can read it. It is never uploaded.

TARGET's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your own progress between your own computers. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Removing your data

In TARGET, Stop syncing in Settings signs that computer out. To remove the copy in Drive, delete the "TARGET sync" folder from your Drive. To remove TARGET's access completely, go to your Google account's permissions page. To remove your entries from a computer, delete the Target folder in %APPDATA%.
